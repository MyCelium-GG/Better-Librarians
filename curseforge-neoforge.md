# Better Librarians (Neoforge)

Look at a librarian, see their books. A small title card lists every
enchanted book a villager trades - no trading GUI, no hovering required.
Rename villagers with `/vname` (legacy `&` codes + `&#RRGGBB` RGB) for
floating in-world names.

- Crosshair title card: profession, level and each enchanted book
- Gold for max-level, white otherwise; catalogued on first trade, persisted

Requires **Mycel**. Needs NeoForge, Minecraft 26.3, 26.2 or 26.1.2, Java 25.
