# Better Librarians

Look at a librarian, see their books. A small title card lists every
enchanted book a villager trades - no trading GUI, no hovering required.
Rename villagers with colorful floating names. Client side, no keybinds.

- Crosshair title card: profession, level and each enchanted book (`Sharpness V`)
- Gold for max-level books, white otherwise
- Villagers are catalogued on first trade; data persists across restarts
- `/vname <name>` renames the villager you look at (`&` legacy codes + `&#RRGGBB` RGB), `/vname clear` removes it
- Config: `enabled`, `showLevel`, `highlightMaxLevel`, `maxBooks`, `showUncataloguedHint`
- Requires **Mycel**: install it alongside this mod

Reload live with `/mycel reload betterlibrarians`.
