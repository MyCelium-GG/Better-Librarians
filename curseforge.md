# Better Librarians

Look at a librarian, see their books and prices on a small card, with no trading screen. Then press K to search everyone you've traded with: type "mending" to see who sells it, cheapest first, and make them glow so you can find them.

**New in 1.3.0, the restock timer:** see which trades are sold out and exactly when a villager restocks, right on the card and under the trading screen. It also warns you when a villager can't restock at all, like a trading hall cell built too far from its lectern. Works for every working villager, not only librarians.

![A librarian's card: Mending 24 and Sharpness V 31 emeralds, 2 trades sold out, Restocks in 0:43](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/betterlibrarians/img/restock-card.png?v=130)

![A librarian whose lectern is 4 blocks away: Can't restock here](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/betterlibrarians/img/restock-too-far.png?v=130)

- Restock timer: sold-out trades and the time until the next restock, exact with the mod on the server (always in singleplayer), an estimate on other servers
- Warnings when a villager has no workstation or stands too far from it
- Card with profession, level, each enchanted book and its emerald price
- Gold for max-level books, white for the rest
- Librarian list on K or `/librarians`, nearest first, with distance, direction and stock
- Villagers are catalogued the first time you open their trades, and the data survives restarts
- Kept per world and server, so lists don't mix
- `/vname <name>` renames the villager you look at (`&` codes and `&#RRGGBB`), `/vname clear` removes it

## Install

Install it in your own game. Put it on the server too for exact restock times on that server; without it there you get an estimate.

In 11 languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Italian, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

Requires [Mycel](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mycel): install it alongside this mod. Minecraft 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3 on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, Java 25. On Fabric you also need Fabric API.

Every setting and command is on the [wiki](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/betterlibrarians/).
